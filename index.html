FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

Can't find what you need? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are printed within 2–4 working days and then reach you in about 3–7 working days, depending on your location. You'll get tracking details once your order ships.

Do you ship across India?

Yes. We ship to most serviceable pin codes across India through trusted courier partners. If your pin code isn't serviceable, we'll let you know before you pay.

What if my order arrives damaged or wrong?

We'll make it right. Write to us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we'll arrange a free replacement or refund for damaged, defective or incorrect items. Personalised items can't be returned for change of mind. See Shipping & Returns.

Can I personalise a product with my own design?

Absolutely. Add names, text, photos or your own artwork. Please only upload content you have the rights to use. Need help? Our design team can create something original for you.

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Yes. We handle team tees, event merch, welcome kits and corporate gift sets. Share your quantity, timeline and idea through the contact form for a quote.

How can I pay?

We accept secure online payments. Payment details are shared with your order confirmation or quote.

How do I contact you?

Email us at karmyxbusiness@gmail.com, call +91 63976 04558, or use the contact form below. We usually reply within 1–2 working days.
