Help

Still stuck? Email karmyxbusiness@gmail.com or call +91 63976 04558. We reply within 1 to 2 working days.

Which size should I order?

Both pieces are cut oversized. Take your usual size for the loose, dropped-shoulder look. Size down once if you want it closer to the body.

How long does delivery take?

Printing takes 2 to 4 working days, then delivery takes about 3 to 7 working days depending on your pin code. You get tracking once it ships.

Do you ship everywhere in India?

We ship to most serviceable pin codes. If yours isn't covered, checkout will tell you before you pay.

What if it arrives damaged or wrong?

Email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we'll send a free replacement or refund. Details are on Shipping and returns.

How do I wash it?

Inside out, cold water. Don't iron directly on the print and skip high-heat tumble drying. The print will hold its colour for a long time.
